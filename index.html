How joining works

We keep the group small and friendly, so every application gets read by a real person.

1

Tell us about yourself

Fill the short form below — who you are, what your ideal weekend looks like, how you heard about us.

2

We read every application

One of us goes through it (usually within a few days). We're not gatekeeping — we're just keeping things safe and cozy.

3

You get the invite

Once you're in, you'll get an email with the community link and details for the next event. See you there.
